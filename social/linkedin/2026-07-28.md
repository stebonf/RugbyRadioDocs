The Most Important Person Using Rugby Radio Live Isn't the Commentator.
When people see Rugby Radio Live, they usually focus on the commentator.
The person tapping the events.

But that's not who I built it for.
I built it for the parent stuck at work.

For the grandparent who can't travel.
For friends following the match from another city.
For everyone who would otherwise receive just one message:
"We won."

A rugby match is much more than the final score.
It's the story behind it.

Every try.
Every comeback.
Every yellow card.
Every moment of tension.

Technology is only valuable if it helps people feel closer to the moments that matter.

That's still the goal of Rugby Radio Live.
Not building another sports app.
Helping people stay connected to the people they care about.
