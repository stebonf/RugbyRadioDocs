Meet Zoe

Not every rugby fan is an expert.
And that's perfectly fine.
One thing I noticed while building Rugby Radio Live is that many people following a match are experiencing rugby for the first time.

Parents.
Grandparents.
Friends.

People who don't always know why the referee stopped the game or what just happened on the field.

That's why I created Zoe.
Zoe's mission isn't to comment on the match.
It's to make rugby easier to understand.

She explains the game, answers common questions and helps new supporters enjoy every moment without feeling lost.
Because growing the rugby community doesn't just mean bringing more people to the field.

It also means helping them understand what they're watching.

🏉 Welcome to the team, Zoe.
