Meet Vox

Many people think Rugby Radio Live is just a platform.
In reality, it's a team.
The only difference is that some team members are not human.

The first one to join was Vox.
Vox is an AI Talker: it takes match events and turns them into engaging live commentary.

Behind every comment is a Large Language Model that interprets what is happening on the field and transforms structured data into natural language.
The goal was never to generate a simple automated summary.

The challenge was to turn a single click — a try, a penalty, a substitution — into something that feels like a real voice following the game.
For me, Vox is more than a feature.
It's an experiment in how AI can bridge the gap between raw data and human storytelling.
Because between an event being recorded and a story being told, there is far more technology than most people imagine.

🎙️ Meet Vox.
The first AI Talker of Rugby Radio Live.
